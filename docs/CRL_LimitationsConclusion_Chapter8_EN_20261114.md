# Limitations and Conclusion (Chapter 8, formal English)

Working title: *Identifiability Boundaries and Conditional Recovery of Covariance-Based Environments*
Date: 2026-11-14. Status: formal manuscript section. The limitations state the boundaries of the claims; the conclusion restates only what the evidence supports.

---

## 8.1 Limitations

**The compatibility premise is assumed, not derived.** The central bridge is a recording-level compatibility condition between the latent assignment and the population K-means objective. It is an explicit hypothesis, and the closed level-set condition and the quadratic-growth condition are hypotheses as well. Nothing in the generative model forces them to hold, and the earlier exchange argument that sought to derive them is withdrawn, with the reason recorded. Proving the alignment on a structured class of distributions, where the environment assignment of every center is well defined and split-merge exchanges can be controlled, is the natural next step and lies beyond this paper. The quantitative growth bound is local, confined to a stated range of the misclassification level, and no global version is claimed.

**The number of environments is not identified.** Every theoretical statement conditions on $K$. There is no proof that $K$ is identifiable or consistently estimable, and the blind selector under-counts on the pooled real data, a stable finding rather than an isolated miss. Automatic selection, full-pool standardization, and finite-initialization Lloyd stay outside the unconditional guarantees, and the bound that does hold is for the global empirical solution under a fixed transformation. The finite-sample theory addresses a fixed-window target, and the window-level and recording-level estimands coincide only under extra conditions.

**Several technical pieces remain open.** An explicit, algorithm-independent bound on the training-side center error in the sample-split decomposition is not established, and the projection cross-terms that appear when the centers are estimated are treated through an all-direction control rather than a closed form. The exact correspondence between the estimated and population objects, the unification of the scaler coordinates, and the fitted center term are each stated as conditions. The K-means basin of attraction and a local-to-global proof for the non-split algorithm are not provided. On the data side, bad-channel interpolation and an absolute quality threshold are absent, and the channel-sensitivity result rules out only a single-channel explanation, not distributed artifacts or a shared device fingerprint.

**The empirical and identification claims are bounded.** The real-data label is a technical mixture of device, task, population, duration, reference, and preprocessing, and its driver cannot be separated from the pooled data. No biological or causal reading is available, and the three-source design gives only three clusters, which is repeatability evidence rather than a generality proof. Full cross-domain generalization is not established; the leave-one-source study supports novelty detection, not correct labeling of a new environment. The partial-identification framing is descriptive rather than a proved sharp set, and the resampling intervals that accompany it are finite-sample diagnostics, not an unconditional coverage theorem; the effective sample size used in the rates is a diagnostic quantity. The general non-Gaussian, fully blind identification problem remains open, and the fixed-shape recovery that does work is a parametric assumption on the same footing as the Gaussian, not a relaxation.

## 8.2 Conclusion

The paper set out to ask whether a covariance-based environment structure stays identifiable once the labels disappear and the observations are pooled. The answer is deliberately several answers rather than one.

A concrete two-dimensional witness shows that two genuinely different partitions can induce the same pooled distribution, enough to rule out treating identifiability as automatic without making a universal claim. Tighten the setting to a constrained, whitened two-component frame and the component covariance pair is point-identified, with every assumption shown; the result is a constrained special case, and the moment-order threshold beyond it is reported as cited work rather than repackaged. Relax the assumptions and the target can only be described as a set of consistent structures, a descriptive partial-identification framing rather than a proved sharp set, with resampling intervals that carry no coverage guarantee.

The practical contribution is a recording-level analysis framework that keeps the observation mechanism, the estimand, and the algorithm version distinct. Under a fixed number of environments and a fixed transformation, the global empirical K-means solution carries a finite-sample risk-gap guarantee, and a sample-split variant gives a held-out assignment decomposition whose training-side term stays explicit rather than hidden. The standard components are not presented as novel parts; the contribution lies in the estimand, the conditional guarantee, and the repeatable failure boundary.

On three independent EEG sources, the same story is checked end to end. Source-associated structure is recoverable under a known $K$ and a balanced design, and the conclusion survives subject-level resampling. The blind selector under-counts, the unbalanced plug-in is weak, and within-source recovery is absent, all reported as findings rather than buried. They describe the technical, dataset-level structure defined in Section 7.1.

The open problems are where the work points next. Deriving the compatibility condition from a structured distribution class, establishing the identifiability of the number of environments, closing the training-side bound in the sample-split result, and extending the framework to non-Gaussian and genuinely unseen settings would each move the boundary outward. Until then, the claims are offered with their conditions attached, and the distinctions drawn here are intended to make clear exactly which downstream uses can be trusted.
